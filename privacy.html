pbcut privacy policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026.

pbcut is a personal tool used only by its owner to manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channel.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, pbcut uses the YouTube Data API to upload the owner's own videos and to set their titles, descriptions, chapters and privacy (unlisted). It reads the status of those videos to confirm they finished processing.

What it stores

The sign-in token and all video files stay on the owner's own computer. pbcut does not collect data from anyone else, has no other users, and does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's security settings (third-party access).

Contact

Questions can be raised as an issue on this project's GitHub repository.
